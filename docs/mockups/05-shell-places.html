Mockups / Phase 2 · App shell & places

App shell and places

A persistent place rail and channel sidebar on wide screens, a tab bar and stacked screens on phones. The command palette is the fastest way to move around on desktop: it searches places, channels, topics and people at once, and every row shows the keys that get there.

Desktop shell

Three columns: place rail, the place's boards and channels, then content. Unread counts are the only white fill in the shell, so they read as the thing to act on.

Phone

The place rail becomes the first tab. Opening a place pushes its boards and channels as a full screen; the tab bar stays so notifications and search are one tap away.
