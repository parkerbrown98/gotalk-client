Forums: threads, search and inbox

The forum screens that sit around the topic list and the flat topic view: replies in threaded boards, the edit history of a post, starting a topic, searching a place with filters, and the inbox that collects replies, mentions and accepted answers. Every action here appears only when the person's permissions allow it, and watch levels (watching, normal, muted) are set where the thing lives: the board, the topic, or the place.
