Welcome and sign in

One screen instead of three. Step one picks a server, with Gotalk Official (api.gotalk.sh) offered first; step two signs in or creates an account there, with the chosen server pinned above the form. Nothing is saved to the device until someone signs in, creates an account or chooses to browse. The app icon appears twice: small beside the wordmark, and as Gotalk Official's own icon. Its glow is the only red on the screen.

Phone

The same two steps. The wordmark replaces the title bar on step one; step two gets a back chevron that returns to the server choice.
