Push notifications

Settings > Notifications, for the active instance. Turning push on is a choice per device and instance: it asks for permission once (from a tap, as browsers require) and registers this device with the instance's push gateway. The categories belong to the account and apply to all its devices. The list shows the devices that get notifications, with this one marked. Every push names its instance, and tapping one opens the conversation, channel message, topic or place it is about.
